## Axis 4 — Modality as a first-class, pluggable choice

### A4-Q16 — SHARPENED · time-box: 1-2 days

**Unknown.** Two separable unknowns, not one. (U1, regime) Under a REAL Helix steady state (`%` then `s\w+<CR>` producing N non-overlapping selections), when one motion keystroke (`w`/`e` extend-all) is applied, what is the realized size of `overlap_indices` per keystroke — does it stay small/local (keeping merge_overlapping_selections at O(N*k), effectively linear) or chain to ~N (collapsing to the O(N^2) Vec::contains)? This is pure runtime behavior of real motions on real content and is the load-bearing branch. (U2, constant factor) For whichever regime U1 lands in, what is the crossover N at which p50 of (merge + navigate + normalize_selections + full to_rendered_selection_set rebuild) exceeds 16.67ms (and the 8ms input-latency target), on a markdown/code buffer (not lorem-ipsum), for a model pre-aged with dead anchors? Answer shape: "non-overlapping steady-state stays < 16.67ms to N >= X; realized overlap_indices stays < k under real `w` motions so the quadratic is not reached at the 50k worst case" confirms the model layer; "realized overlap_indices chains to ~N and crosses budget below N ~= 5k" refutes and forces the HashSet fix + cap. Explicitly OUT of this spike's scope (must not be cleared by its green): the layout+paint frame cost.

**Hypothesis.** The non-overlapping steady-state path is O(N log N) per keystroke (merge's sort at buffer.rs:1318 + the early-return at selection_model.rs:161 skipping the quadratic + flat O(N) navigate/normalize/render passes) and stays under 16.67ms to roughly N ~= 10k-50k; resolve is O(1) (anchor.rs:209, confirmed). The kill risk is NOT a synthetic all-colliding geometry (that quadratic is already known by reading + arithmetic: at N=50k all-overlapping it is ~25x over budget, so it WILL blow up if reached) — the real question is whether real `w`/`e` extend motions chain adjacent selections into one giant OverlapRange (making overlap_indices ~= N and triggering the O(N^2) contains, AND incidentally collapsing many selections — a likely correctness bug too) or keep overlaps local. I expect real motions to produce mostly-local overlaps (overlap_indices small), so the steady-state model path stays sub-quadratic and within budget at N=50k — BUT this must be measured, not assumed, and the verdict cannot extend to the full frame because paint of 50k quads is unmeasured and plausibly the real bottleneck. So: pluggable multi-selection is likely viable at the MODEL layer; the HashSet fix in merge_overlapping_selections is mandatory-regardless cheap insurance; a selection-count cap and incremental/dirty-region render remain live and are gated on the paint measurement, not on this bench.

_Why code can't settle it:_ duplicate-removed

**The spike:** Throwaway criterion bench in crates/editor that (Stage 1) drives a REAL `%`+`s\w+`-style selection split on a real markdown/code buffer to produce N non-overlapping selections, then applies one real `w`/`e` extend-all keystroke via extend_selection and instruments the REALIZED overlap_indices size; (Stage 2) sweeps N and times the model-update + full to_rendered_selection_set rebuild against 16.67ms/8ms, on a model pre-aged with dead anchors and an interleaved edit to expose the retain spike. Explicitly does NOT claim to measure the frame; paint is flagged out-of-scope.

- _Touch points:_ `NEW crates/editor/benches/multi_selection_keystroke.rs (clone buffer_bench.rs scaffold; criterion 0.5.1 + [[bench]] already wired Cargo.toml:64-67; reuse mock_buffer at buffer_bench.rs:16 with a REAL markdown/code fixture, not lorem-ipsum)`, `Build the N-selection set by applying a real word-match split (regex over the buffer, set via set_selection_offsets at selection_model.rs:372) — NOT hand-strided ranges — so the geometry reflects real `s\w+` output`, `Exercise the real keystroke path: extend_selection (selection.rs:729) -> update_selections_internal (selection.rs:672) -> merge_overlapping_selections (selection_model.rs:156); time to_rendered_selection_set (buffer.rs:2081)`, `PRIMARY instrument: capture realized overlap_indices.len() per keystroke (selection_model.rs:157) across the sweep — this is the headline output, it tells you whether production is in the O(N) or O(N^2) regime`, `Anchor-bloat realism: use criterion iter_batched, pre-age the model with ~1000 prior set_selection_offsets applies (no edit, so anchor.rs:141 retain never runs) and assert/record Anchors.anchors.len() (anchor.rs:86) growth; interleave ONE text edit to capture the retain-reclaim p99 spike`, `Sweep N in {1,100,1000,5000,10000,50000}; record p50 AND p99 vs 16.67ms and 8ms, separated by realized-overlap regime (small-k vs chained-N)`

- ✅ **Success:** SCOPED TO THE MODEL+SELECTION-REBUILD LAYER ONLY: (a) realized overlap_indices stays small/local (k << N) under real `w`/`e` extend-all on the markdown/code buffer, so merge_overlapping_selections does NOT enter the O(N^2) regime; AND (b) one keystroke (merge + navigate + normalize + full to_rendered_selection_set) p50 < 16.67ms (ideally < 8ms) up to N = 10k and the ~50k `%`+`s\w+` worst case stays < 16.67ms; AND (c) Anchors map growth across 1000 no-edit applies does not regress resolve below O(1) and the next edit's retain reclaims it. This confirms the MODEL layer can carry multi-selection without a cap — but the no-incrementality / no-cap decision for the WHOLE feature stays OPEN pending a separate paint measurement; this bench is not allowed to clear it.
- 🛑 **Kill:** Any of: (1) realized overlap_indices chains to ~N under real `w`/`e` motions (the quadratic Vec::contains in merge_overlapping_selections at selection_model.rs:172/181 is actually reached AND many selections wrongly collapse — a correctness bug too) — forces the HashSet fix + likely a cap; (2) model+render p50 crosses 16.67ms below N ~= 5k in the realistic non-overlapping regime — forces incremental selection-diff + dirty-region render before the IR ossifies; (3) Anchors map grows unbounded across the 1000 no-edit applies such that resolve locality degrades measurably (set_selection_offsets mints 2N anchors/call, anchor.rs:141 retain runs only on edits) — forces a prune-on-set or a model-rebuild-on-large-set strategy. Note: the synthetic all-colliding worst case is NOT a valid kill on its own — reading + arithmetic already prove it is ~25x over budget at 50k, so it only matters if signal (1) shows real motions actually reach it.
- ⚠️ **False-green risk:** Hardened. A green can still mask production jank because: (1) MODEL-ONLY SCOPE — the bench omits GPUI layout+paint of N selection quads (table.rs:493/595 loop per selection with per-cell offset mapping), autoscroll, and full SelectionChanged subscriber fan-out; a green here is necessary-not-sufficient for the 16.67ms frame and must NOT be read as 'no incrementality needed'. (2) WRONG-PROXY GEOMETRY (the single most likely false green, now corrected) — both original geometries are invalid proxies: evenly-strided non-overlapping never reaches the quadratic (green but irrelevant because merge short-circuits at selection_model.rs:161), and hand-forced all-colliding ranges trigger a quadratic that real `w`/`e` motions may never produce (red but fictional). The bench MUST derive selections from a real regex split and drive real extend motions, and measure realized overlap_indices — the regime is the answer, not a synthetic worst case. (3) WARM-CACHE / CLEAN-MODEL — criterion's default amortized mean on a freshly-sized HashMap hides dead-anchor bloat (anchor.rs:141 prunes only on edit); use iter_batched with a pre-aged model + interleaved edit to expose retain-spike p99. (4) SINGLE-KEYSTROKE — users hold autorepeat (~30/s); each repeat re-merges + re-renders, so sustained input multiplies cost; report a held-repeat burst, not one op. (5) PLAIN-TEXT — lorem-ipsum skips inline_style_boundary_at / rich active_style reduce that scale with content; a markdown/code fixture is mandatory.
- 💡 _Cheaper alternative:_ Partial — a grep + Fermi estimate (now done) already settles roughly half the question for free: reading confirms merge_overlapping_selections is O(N^2) only when overlap_indices ~= N (Vec::contains, selection_model.rs:172/181, gated by the is_empty early-return at :161), and arithmetic (N^2/2 usize comparisons vs a 16.67ms budget at sub-ns/compare gives crossover ~10k; at the 50k all-overlapping worst case ~25x over budget) proves the pathological geometry blows up IF reached. That alone justifies landing the HashSet fix immediately WITHOUT a bench, and means a synthetic all-colliding geometry adds no information. What paper/grep CANNOT settle, and what still justifies a build: (U1) whether real `w`/`e` extend motions actually drive overlap_indices to ~N (the regime question), and (U2) the constant factor of the non-overlapping O(N log N) + full-rebuild render path under a bloated anchor map. So: do the cheap HashSet fix now; run a SLIMMED build focused only on U1 (realized overlap size under real motions) + U2 constant factor — not the redundant synthetic quadratic.
- _Keep vs throw:_ KEEP: the realized-overlap-regime table and the N-vs-latency table as a permanent regression guard wired into the Helix/multi-selection work; KEEP any forced fix as real PRs — (i) swap the two `overlap_indices.contains` in merge_overlapping_selections (selection_model.rs:172/181) for a HashSet membership (mandatory-regardless cheap insurance, target is selection_model.rs NOT overlapping_ranges), (ii) collapse the redundant merge across buffer.rs:1637/1771 and selection.rs:683 if the bench shows it runs more than once per keystroke, (iii) prune-on-set or bound the Anchors map. THROW: the synthetic N-selection construction scaffolding and the ad-hoc anchor-count instrumentation harness.
- _Unblocks:_ A4-Q16 itself (the per-keystroke multi-selection frame-budget question, now split into a settled model-layer answer + an explicitly-deferred paint question); A4-Q6 (a kill on regime/cap forces the costly-to-reverse primary-index + selection-count-cap decision before the IR freezes); the 'Helix as the proof' Phase-3-entry bar in the decision briefs (de-risks selection-set CREATION primitives); A4-Q3 (executor trait surface — establishes whether selection-set creation must carry a max-selections / incrementality contract). - _Residual risk:_ Even with the corrected build: (a) the paint/layout cost of N quads is deliberately unmeasured here and is plausibly the true frame bottleneck — the 'no cap / no incrementality' decision stays open and could still flip the feature toward mandatory incrementality after a separate render spike; (b) the bench's 'real motion' regime depends on the specific test fixture's word density and the specific motion sequence chosen — a pathological real document (dense adjacent words causing chained overlaps) could land in the O(N^2) regime that the chosen fixture misses, so the regime result is fixture-sensitive and should be run over 2-3 representative docs; (c) criterion measures this machine's constants — CI/lower-end hardware shifts the crossover N downward, so the reported N must be treated as an upper bound, not a guarantee; (d) the triple merge call sites (buffer.rs:1637/1771, selection.rs:683) mean the per-keystroke merge multiplier must be confirmed by the bench, not assumed to be exactly two.
- _Red-team:_ Verified every touch_point against source. Four substantive corrections that change whether a green is trustworthy: (1) PROXY/MIS-ATTRIBUTION: the spike pins the O(N^2) on `overlapping_ranges` (buffer.rs:1308), but that function is O(N log N) (sort + one linear pass, no Vec::contains). A model-only green is a NECESSARY-not-sufficient condition for the FULL frame; paint can independently demand incrementality. The conclusion must be scoped down or the green is false. (3) DEEPEST FALSE GREEN — both synthetic geometries are wrong proxies: geometry A never overlaps (green but irrelevant); hand-strided geometry B forces a worst case that real `%`+`s\w+`+`w` motions may never produce (red but possibly fictional). The realized `overlap_indices` size under REAL motions is the actual unknown that decides O(N) vs O(N^2), and neither synthetic geometry nor paper settles it. (4) MEASUREMENT GAMEABILITY: criterion's warm-cache amortized mean on a freshly-built model hides the dead-anchor bloat (anchor.rs:141 retain runs only on edits; set_selection_offsets mints 2N anchors/call) — must use iter_batched with a pre-aged model and an interleaved edit to surface the retain-spike p99.
